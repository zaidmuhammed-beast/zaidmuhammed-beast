/ 01 — About ABS

Nine years of turning plots into landmarks.

ABS Developers is a Lahore-headquartered real estate developer that builds high-rise residential, commercial and mixed-use destinations — and finances every one of them without interest.

From Pearl One Tower, completed and handed over in under twenty months, to Burj Quaid in DHA City Karachi, the approach has stayed the same: pre-plan the whole build, self-finance the construction phase, and give possession when the brochure said we would.
